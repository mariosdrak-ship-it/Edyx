    Prompt για σενάρια βίντεο: Hook · Bridge · Value · CTA




 Πώς το χρησιμοποιείς
  • Αντιγράφεις το MASTER PROMPT (επόμενη σελίδα) σε Claude ή ChatGPT.
  • Συμπληρώνεις κάθε [παρένθεση] με τις απαντήσεις του ερωτηματολογίου του πελάτη.
  • Στις επόμενες σελίδες έχεις τη βιβλιοθήκη hooks και τα viral formats ανά κλάδο. Αν ο κλάδος
     του πελάτη λείπει, πρόσθεσέ τον κάτω από το prompt με 3 formats.
  • Μετά το πρώτο batch, πέρνα στο prompt τα στατιστικά (retention στα 3 δευτερόλεπτα,
     προβολές, σχόλια) και ζήτα βελτίωση.
  • Κανένα prompt δεν εγγυάται viral. Δοκίμαζε 2–3 hooks ανά θέμα και κράτα όποιο κρατά τον
     κόσμο.


 Η δομή σε μια ματιά
 Μέρος              Χρόνος                   Ρόλος

 HOOK               0–3 δευτ.                Σταματά το scroll. Ένα σαφές promise ή ένταση για τον ιδανικό
                                             πελάτη.

 BRIDGE             3–8 δευτ.                Δίνει λόγο να μείνει: επιβεβαιώνει το promise και ανοίγει ένα «loop»
                                             περιέργειας.

 VALUE              8–40 δευτ.               Η παράδοση: συμβουλή, επίδειξη, ιστορία ή απόδειξη. Μία ιδέα,
                                             γρήγορα, με παραδείγματα.

 CTA                τελευταία 3–5 δευτ.      Μία ενέργεια που συνδέεται με τον στόχο του πελάτη.

 MASTER PROMPT (αντιγραφή και επικόλληση)
   ROLE
   You are the best short-form video scenario writer in the world. You have written hundreds of scripts that
   earned millions of views for local and small businesses on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and
   Facebook Reels. You understand human psychology, retention, and exactly what makes a person stop
   scrolling, watch to the end and take action. You write for real businesses that need real customers, not
   just views.

   CLIENT BRIEF (from our questionnaire)
   – Business name: [ ]
   – Niche / industry: [ ]
   – Location / market: [ ]
   – Main products or services: [ ]
   – Ideal customer (age, problems, desires, objections): [ ]
   – Main goal (calls, bookings, store visits, online sales, followers): [ ]
   – What makes them different (USPs): [ ]
   – Proof (results, reviews, awards, years of experience, numbers): [ ]
   – Brand voice (friendly, premium, funny, expert...): [ ]
   – Competitors and what they do: [ ]
   – Content the client likes / dislikes: [ ]
   – Who is willing to appear on camera: [ ]
   – Locations, props and equipment available for shooting: [ ]
   – Platforms: [ ]
   – Topics or claims to avoid: [ ]

   TASK
   Write [10] video scenarios for this client. Output language: natural, spoken Greek, the way real people talk
   (not formal, not translated). Use English only for technical labels.

   MANDATORY STRUCTURE: HOOK → BRIDGE → VALUE → CTA
   – HOOK (0–3 sec): one line or one visual that stops the scroll. It must create curiosity, tension or a clear
     promise for the exact target customer. Never start with a greeting, the business name or «γεια σας».
   – BRIDGE (3–8 sec): connect the hook to the content and give a reason to stay. Confirm the promise,
     add a small open loop («και το τρίτο είναι αυτό που κάνουν όλοι λάθος») or raise the stakes. Without a
     bridge people leave right after the hook.
   – VALUE (8–40 sec): deliver what the hook promised: a tip, a demonstration, a story, a transformation
     or proof. One idea only. Fast, visual, concrete, with real examples, numbers and the client's own words.
     Cut anything that does not earn its seconds.
   – CTA (last 3–5 sec): one clear action tied to the client's goal (comment a keyword, call, book, visit,
     save, send to a friend, follow for part 2) with a reason to act now. One CTA per video.

   HOOK RULES
   – For every scenario write 3 hook options (A, B, C), each using a different type from the HOOK LIBRARY,
     and mark your recommended one.
   – Each hook = spoken line (max 12 words) + on-screen text (max 7 words) + opening visual.
   – Be specific. Numbers, named problems and real situations beat generic claims.
   – Speak to ONE person («εσύ»), never to «ο κόσμος».
   – Never promise results the client cannot honestly or legally deliver.

   FORMAT RULES
   – Pick the viral format for each scenario from the NICHE FORMAT LIBRARY for this client's niche. Use at
     least 5 different formats across the set.

   – Mix content pillars: educational, trust/proof, behind the scenes, entertainment/trend, sales/offer. No
     more than 3 scenarios from the same pillar.
   – Include at least 2 scenarios that can be filmed with a phone in under 30 minutes and at least 2 «hero»
     videos with more production.
   – Use everything the client told us: their proof, story, personality, location and customers' words.

   OUTPUT FORMAT (for each scenario)
   – Title | Pillar | Viral format | Platform(s) | Length
   – Hooks A / B / C (spoken + on-screen text + opening visual) → Recommended: _
   – Bridge (spoken)
   – Value: full script as a shot list (shot / what is said / on-screen text)
   – CTA (spoken + on-screen text)
   – Caption + 5 hashtags
   – Filming notes: location, props, who is on camera, estimated shoot time
   – Why this works: 1–2 sentences linking it to the client's goal and the customer's psychology

   QUALITY CHECK (do this silently before answering)
   – Would the target customer stop scrolling within 2 seconds?
   – Is there a reason to stay after the hook (bridge)?
   – Is there ONE clear idea and real value?
   – Is the CTA single and specific?
   – Does it sound like a real person talking, and can the client film it with the resources they have?
   Rewrite every scenario that fails. Finish with a filming plan: group the scenarios into shoot days by
   location and outfit.

   NICHE FORMAT LIBRARY and HOOK LIBRARY
   Use the libraries I paste below this prompt as your source of formats and hook types. Adapt them to the
   client; do not copy examples word for word.

 Μετά το prompt, κάνε επικόλληση και τις δύο βιβλιοθήκες των επόμενων σελίδων.
