# PROMPT: news-jacking posts ("what is happening in Greece" → ad posts)

1. Search the Greek news of the last 3 days (newspaper front pages, economy, society). Pick 4 stories that matter AND connect to marketing/business.
   Skip: tragedies, deaths, crimes against persons, war casualties, anything that would look like exploiting suffering, dangerous behaviour.
2. For each story give: the fact (with outlet + date), why it matters to a Greek small business, the hook, the 1-line lesson, the picture needed.
3. Wording rules for real cases: «φέρεται», «φερόμενη», «Η υπόθεση είναι σε εξέλιξη». Never name suspects or companies, blur logos/names/faces, never show children.
   We are not lawyers: say so when the topic is legal.
4. Poster layout (4:5 1206x1505): label (gold) · big hook in 3 lines (white / gold italic / white) · small italic line · 3-4 lesson points with gold numerals · CTA right of the logo.
5. Verify numbers on the day of posting. Never invent a statistic.
Examples that worked: electricity bills rising → "Don't cut marketing first"; €2 fee on non-EU parcels → "customers look for Greek e-shops"; Black Friday countdown; pay transparency → "your image as an employer is part of your marketing".
