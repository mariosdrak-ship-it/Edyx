EXACTLY 5 hashtags, on the last line, separated by single spaces:
1. **#edyx** (always, every post)
2-3. two about the job (e.g. #καφετέρια #μινιμαρκετ), written without accents
4-5. two marketing tags (pick from #socialmediamarketing #videomarketing #marketingagency #μικρεςεπιχειρησεις #ελλάδα)
Never more than 5. Never repeat the same 5 on two posts in a row.
